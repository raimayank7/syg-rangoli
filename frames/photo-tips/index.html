Photo Frames

Photo tips for frames, collages and prints

The frame is the easy part. What makes a framed photo or a collage look good is the photo that goes into it. These habits cost nothing and make a clear difference.

Start with the original file

  • Pick the photo from your gallery or camera folder, not a copy that came through a chat. Chat apps shrink photos, and each forward can shrink them again.
  • Never use a screenshot of a photo. A screenshot is only as large as your screen, and it includes none of the original detail.
  • If someone must send you a photo for printing, ask them to send it as a document or with the HD option, or by email.

Taking a photo that frames well

  • Light: face the window or the open door. Light from behind a person turns the face dark.
  • Height: hold the phone at the eye level of the person, especially for children.
  • Space: leave a little room around heads and shoulders. Frames and crops eat the edges.
  • Clean the lens: a wipe with a soft cloth removes the haze that fingerprints leave.
  • Take three: someone always blinks.

Copying an old print with a phone

  1. Take the print out of its frame or album sleeve if you can do so without damage. Glass and plastic reflect light.
  2. Lay it flat near a window, in shade, not in direct sun.
  3. Hold the phone exactly above it, parallel to the print, so the edges of the print line up with the edges of the screen.
  4. Turn the flash off. Tap the screen on the print to focus, and take the picture.
  5. Crop away the table. Many phones have a "scan" or "document" mode in the camera or the gallery that straightens the picture for you.

Small edits that help

  • Straighten: a level horizon or door frame makes any photo look more careful.
  • Crop: remove empty space and distractions at the edges.
  • Brightness: lift a dark photo a little. Stop before the white areas lose their detail.
  • Leave filters for last: and use the same one on every photo in a collage.

Keeping photos safe

  • Keep two copies in two places: for example the phone and a computer, or the phone and an online backup.
  • Name folders by year and event, such as "2026-11 Diwali".
  • Print the few that matter most. A print in a frame is seen every day, and it does not need a password.
