Pixels are for 300 dots per inch, the usual standard for a sharp photo print. Shape is the ratio of the short side to the long side.

How large can my photo be printed?

To find the pixel size of a photo on a phone, open it in the gallery and look under Details or Info.

Choosing a size

  • Phone photos are usually 3:4. They fit 6 × 8 inch paper exactly and lose a little on 4 × 6 and 5 × 7.
  • Camera photos are usually 2:3. They fit 4 × 6 and 8 × 12 exactly.
  • Square photos need square prints, or a white border on the two short sides.
  • For frames, buy the frame first or check its opening. The visible part is a little smaller than the print.

Before you send photos to print

  • Send the original file. Photos passed through chat apps are often made smaller on the way.
  • Do not enlarge a small photo and expect detail. Making the file bigger does not add sharpness.
  • Ask the shop whether they print "full frame" or "fill the paper". Fill the paper crops the edges.
  • Keep faces and text away from the edges. Printers trim a few millimetres.

Questions people ask

How many pixels do I need for a 4 × 6 print?

1200 × 1800 pixels gives a sharp print at 300 dots per inch. About 800 × 1200 is still acceptable.

What does 300 dpi mean?

Dots per inch: how many pixels are printed along one inch of paper. 300 looks sharp when held in the hand. Large prints seen from a distance can use 150 or even 100.

Why is part of my photo cut off in the print?

The shape of the photo and the shape of the paper differ. A phone photo is usually 3:4, and a 4 × 6 print is 2:3, so a strip is trimmed from the long sides. Crop the photo yourself first to choose what is lost.

What is the size of a passport photo in India?

For an Indian passport the photo is 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm). Many other forms ask for 3.5 × 4.5 cm. Always check the instructions of the form you are filling.
