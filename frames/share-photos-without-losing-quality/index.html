How to share photos without losing quality

You take a sharp photo, send it to the family group, and a cousin prints it. The print is soft. Nothing went wrong with the camera or the printer. The photo was made smaller on its way through the chat app. This page explains why that happens and how to send a photo so that the other person receives the same file you have.

Why sent photos get smaller

Chat apps are built to be fast on slow connections. When you send a photo the normal way, the app shrinks it and squeezes it harder before sending. On a phone screen you cannot tell. On a large screen or on paper you can: fine detail such as hair, jewellery and text on a signboard turns smooth or blocky.

Each forward can repeat the process. A photo that has travelled through three groups has been squeezed three times.

When it does not matter

For a status, a greeting or a picture that will only ever be seen on phones, the normal way of sending is fine. Frames and collages from this site are 1080 pixels wide, which suits phone screens, and they survive normal sending well.

When it matters

  • The photo will be printed, especially larger than 4 × 6 inches.
  • Someone will crop one person out of a group photo.
  • It is a photo of a document and the small text must stay readable.
  • You are collecting photos from relatives for an album, a collage or a wedding slideshow.

Ways to send the full file

1. The HD option

WhatsApp shows an HD choice when you select a photo to send. It sends a larger version than the standard one. It is the quickest improvement and is good enough for small prints.

2. Send as a document

In the attach menu, choose Document instead of Gallery, then pick the photo from your files. The app then treats the photo like any other file and sends it unchanged. This is the method to use for anything that will be printed large.

3. Email

Attach the photo to an email. If the mail app asks which size to send, choose "Actual size" or "Original". Email is still the simplest way to reach a photo shop.

4. A cloud link

For many photos at once, such as a whole event, upload them to a cloud folder (Google Photos, Google Drive, iCloud or OneDrive) and share the link. Check the sharing setting: "anyone with the link" for a short time, then switch the link off when everyone has downloaded.

5. Nearby sharing

When the other person is in the same room, phone-to-phone sharing sends the original file without using data: Quick Share on Android phones and AirDrop between Apple devices.

6. Cable or pen drive

For a photo shop, the old ways are the surest. Connect the phone by cable, or copy the photos to a pen drive.

Collecting photos from relatives

If you are gathering photos for a collage or album, say exactly what you need in one message. For example: "Please send 2 or 3 photos with Dadi. Send them as Document, not as normal photos, so that they stay clear for printing." People are glad to help when the steps are spelled out.

How to check what you received

Open the photo's details in the gallery (usually under Info or Details) and look at the pixel size. Then compare it with the megapixels and print size chart, or type the numbers into the checker on the print sizes page. If a photo that should be 3000 × 4000 arrives as 1200 × 1600, ask for it again as a document.

Do not try to "increase" quality afterwards

Enlarging a small photo in an editing app makes the file bigger, not sharper. The detail removed on the way cannot be put back by resizing. The fix is always to go back to the person who has the original.

A word on privacy

A full-size original can carry hidden details, such as the date and sometimes the place where it was taken. That is harmless within the family. Before sending originals to people you do not know well, check your phone's sharing options for a setting that removes location details.

Questions people ask

Why do photos become blurry on WhatsApp?

The app makes photos smaller so that they send quickly. Use the HD option, or send the photo as a document to keep the original file.

How do I send a photo as a document?

In the chat, tap the attach button, choose Document instead of Gallery and select the photo from your files. It is sent unchanged.

Can I restore the quality of a compressed photo?

No. Detail that was removed cannot be recovered by enlarging the file. Ask the sender for the original photo again.
