Photo frame ideas by occasion

A frame should say what the day is before anyone reads a word. This guide matches each of the 22 frames in our frame maker to the occasions it suits, and suggests the kind of photo, the words and the size that work best. Every link opens the frame maker with that frame already chosen.

Birthday

Frame: Birthday, or Round: rainbow ring for the birthday person's own DP. Photo: a close, happy photo of the person alone. A cake photo with ten heads around it is better kept for a collage.

Words: the frame starts with "Happy Birthday". Add the name, or the name and the age for milestone years: "Happy 60th Birthday, Papa". Size: Portrait for a post, Story for status.

Wedding anniversary

Frame: Hearts for a young couple, Classic gold for parents and grandparents. Photo: the couple together, close. An old wedding photo copied with a phone works beautifully in the gold frame.

Words: "Happy 25th Anniversary" with both names, or only the years: "1996 to 2026".

Wedding and engagement

Frame: Wedding, which starts with "Shubh Vivah". Change the words to the couple's names and the date to make a simple save-the-date picture. Photo: one where both faces are near the middle, because the decorated border takes a little of each side. Size: Portrait for sharing in family groups.

New baby

Frame: New baby or Soft pastel. Photo: the baby asleep, or a hand holding a finger, if the parents prefer not to show the face. Words: the name and the date of birth. Ask the parents before you post a newborn's photo anywhere.

Results, promotions and new beginnings

Frame: Congratulations. Photo: the person on the day: in the school uniform, holding the certificate, at the new shop. Words: say what happened in five words: "Class 12, 94 percent", "First day at work".

Farewell and retirement

Frame: Gallery black or Wooden, both calm and formal. Words: the name and the years of service. If you plan to print and gift it, choose the 4 × 6 inch print size and read our guide to frame and mount sizes.

Festivals

Frames: Diwali, Holi colours, Eid, Christmas and Tricolour for the national days. Photo: the family in festival clothes, taken in daylight before the evening begins. See festival photo frames for wording and timing.

In memory

Frame: In loving memory. Photo: a clear, dignified portrait. Words: the name and the years. Our guide to a memorial photo covers the details with care.

No occasion at all

The simple frames are for ordinary good photos. White border makes any photo look printed. Instant photo leaves a wide strip at the bottom for a handwritten-style line. Film strip suits travel and college photos, and Flowers suits portraits of mothers, grandmothers and little girls who asked for flowers.

Three rules that hold for every occasion

  1. Let the frame match the mood of the photo. A loud frame on a quiet photo fights with it.
  2. Write fewer words than you want to. A name and a date outlast a long wish.
  3. Make it once, in the right shape. Choose the size for where it will go, so that nothing is cut later.

Questions people ask

Which frame is best for a birthday photo?

The Birthday frame for a post or status, and the round rainbow ring if the picture will be used as a profile picture.

Can I change the words on an occasion frame?

Yes. Each occasion frame starts with a few words, such as Happy Birthday. Type your own in the box, in any language, or clear the box for no words.

Which frame suits a photo that will be printed?

The simple frames: White border, Classic gold, Gallery black or Wooden, in the 4 × 6 inch print size.
