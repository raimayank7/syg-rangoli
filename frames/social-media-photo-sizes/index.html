Three shapes cover almost everything

  • Square (1:1), 1080 × 1080: profile pictures and safe for any feed.
  • Portrait (4:5), 1080 × 1350: feed posts that should stand out.
  • Upright (9:16), 1080 × 1920: status, stories and reels.

The frame maker and the collage maker on this site offer all three.

Profile pictures are circles

WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and most other services show the profile picture as a circle. The corners of your square photo are hidden, so keep the face in the middle and leave space around it. The round frames in our frame maker put a coloured ring just inside the circle, where it stays visible.

Keep text in the safe area

On stories and status, the top and bottom of the screen are covered by names, buttons and the reply box. Keep words and faces in the middle two-thirds of the picture.

Sending without losing quality

Chat apps shrink photos to save data. WhatsApp offers an HD option when you send a photo, and sending the picture as a document keeps the original file. Use one of these when the picture will be printed.

Questions people ask

What size is a WhatsApp DP?

WhatsApp shows the profile picture as a circle cut from a square. Any square photo of 500 × 500 pixels or more works. Keep the face in the centre.

What size is a WhatsApp status?

A full upright phone screen: 1080 × 1920 pixels, a 9:16 shape.

Which Instagram size takes the most space?

The portrait post, 1080 × 1350 (4:5). It fills more of the screen than a square post as people scroll.

Will my picture be cut?

If its shape does not match, yes. Each service crops to its own shape. Make the picture in the right shape first, so that you choose what is cut.
