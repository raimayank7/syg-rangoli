Wedding anniversary photo ideas

An anniversary picture has one job: to show two people and the time they have shared. The best ones are simple. Here are six ideas that work for a first anniversary as well as a fiftieth, with the frame or layout to use for each.

1. Then and now

Place the wedding-day photo beside a photo taken this year, in the two-photo layout of the collage maker. If you can, take the new photo in the same pose: standing on the same sides, the same hand on the shoulder. Add only the two years at the bottom, for example "2001 and 2026".

2. One photo, well framed

If there is one photo the couple loves, do not dilute it. Put it in the Hearts frame for a young couple or the Classic gold frame for parents, add the names, and stop there.

3. The first year in four photos

For a first anniversary, choose four moments from the year: the wedding, the first trip, the first festival at home, an ordinary Sunday. A four-box grid with thin white spacing looks like an album page.

4. Silver jubilee: the family that grew

For 25 years, use a layout with one large box and smaller boxes around it. The couple's wedding photo goes in the large box. The smaller boxes hold the children at different ages, the first home, and a photo of the couple today. The picture reads from the centre outwards, the way the family grew.

5. Golden jubilee: three generations

For 50 years, a nine-photo grid can hold the couple in the centre, their children in the boxes beside them and grandchildren in the corners. Crop every face to a similar size. Print this one: at A4 size in a real frame, it is a gift that will stay on the wall.

6. From the children, in two lines

A photo of the parents alone, in a quiet frame such as Wooden, with a caption from the children. Our own lines, free to use:

  • Thirty years, and still holding hands.
  • Our first home was the two of you.
  • Happy Anniversary, Mummy and Papa.

Working with old wedding photos

Wedding photos from before phone cameras live in albums, often under a plastic sheet. To copy one:

  1. Lift the plastic sheet if it lifts easily. Do not peel a print that is stuck to the page.
  2. Lay the album near a window in shade and hold the phone flat above the photo.
  3. Turn the flash off and take two or three shots.
  4. Crop to the photo's edges. A slightly faded photo looks right for its age, so do not push the colours too far.

In a then-and-now picture, give the old photo the same size as the new one even if it is less sharp. The contrast is the charm.

Sharing and printing

  • For status: Story size. Add it early in the morning so that relatives see it through the day.
  • For the family group: Portrait size, sent with the HD option so that faces stay clear.
  • As a gift: choose the 4 × 6 print size for a table frame, or build the collage in Portrait and print at 8 × 10 inches for the wall. See printing a collage and frame sizes.

A note on surprises

If the picture is a surprise from the children, collect the photos quietly from an aunt, an uncle or the family group's media folder. Check each photo with one elder before printing: families have photos that carry stories you may not know.

Questions people ask

Which frame suits an anniversary photo?

Hearts for a young couple and Classic gold for parents and grandparents. Both leave space at the bottom for names or years.

How do I make a then and now photo?

Add the old photo and the new photo to the collage maker, pick the two-photo layout, and drag each photo so the faces sit at the same height.

What should I write on an anniversary collage?

Keep it short: the two names, the years, or a five-word line. Longer wishes belong in the message, not on the picture.
