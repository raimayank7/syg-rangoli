Birthday photo frame and collage guide

A birthday picture is the most common reason people open a frame or collage maker, usually at eleven at night. This guide gets you from a gallery full of photos to a finished picture in about ten minutes, and it works whether the birthday is a baby's first or a grandmother's eightieth.

First decide: one photo or many

  • One photo with a frame is right when you have one really good picture, or when the post is from you to one person. Use the Birthday frame.
  • A collage is right when the story is time: how a child has grown, years of friendship, a parent through the decades. Use the collage maker.

Choosing the photos

Go through the gallery once and mark more photos than you need, then cut. Keep the ones where the birthday person looks like themselves, not the ones where you look good. For a collage, choose photos from different times and places so that the boxes do not all look the same.

Ask an elder for old prints if the person is over forty. A school photo or a wedding-day photo, copied with a phone, is the box everyone will talk about. Our photo tips explain how to copy a print without glare.

Collage layouts by age

  • First birthday: the newborn photo in the large box and three or four small boxes of the months after it.
  • Children: one photo from each birthday so far. Up to nine years fits a three-by-three grid exactly.
  • Teenagers and friends: four photos with no spacing and a black background look like a poster. Check with the friend before posting an awkward old photo.
  • Parents: "then and now" in two boxes, or a row of decades: childhood, college, wedding, today.
  • Grandparents: the person in the middle box of a nine-photo grid, with children and grandchildren around them.

Words that fit on a frame

The caption has room for about six words. These are our own short lines, free to use:

  • Happy Birthday, Riya. Stay loud.
  • Sixty years young, Papa.
  • One year of you, a lifetime to go.
  • Happy Birthday to our favourite teacher.
  • जन्मदिन मुबारक, दादी माँ
  • ਜਨਮਦਿਨ ਮੁਬਾਰਕ, ਵੀਰੇ

For longer wishes, post the words as the message and keep the picture clean. The sister site Wishes has birthday wishes to copy.

Sizes for where it will go

  • WhatsApp status and Instagram story: Story size, 9:16. Keep faces in the middle, away from the top and bottom.
  • Family group or Instagram post: Portrait, 4:5.
  • Profile picture for the day: Square, with a round ring frame.
  • Printed as a card or gift: the 4 × 6 inch print size. See printing a collage before you go to the shop.

Mistakes that spoil a birthday picture

  • Too many photos. Nine tiny boxes on a phone screen show nobody clearly. Five is plenty.
  • Screenshots. A screenshot of a video call or of an old status is small and soft. Find the real photo.
  • Faces cut by borders. After choosing a layout, tap each box and drag the photo until the whole face is inside.
  • Posting a child's photo publicly. Status and family groups are fine. For public pages, think about who can see it.

If the birthday is today

Pick one photo, the Birthday frame, the name, Portrait size, Download. That takes two minutes, and a simple picture sent in the morning is worth more than a perfect collage sent the next day.

Questions people ask

How many photos should a birthday collage have?

Three to five is best for a phone screen. Use nine only when each box is one year of a child's life, where the count itself is the point.

Can I write the wish in Hindi or Punjabi?

Yes. Type in any language your keyboard supports and the words are drawn on the frame.

How do I make a birthday status?

Choose the Story size in the frame maker or collage maker, keep faces in the middle of the picture, download it and add it to your status.
