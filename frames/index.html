How it works

  1. Open the frame maker or the collage maker.
  2. Choose photos from your phone or computer. They are opened by your browser and are not uploaded.
  3. Pick a frame or a layout and adjust the photo with your finger.
  4. Download the picture or share it to WhatsApp.

What you can make

  • A greeting from your own photo: a birthday, anniversary or festival picture that people open because they know the faces. See frame ideas by occasion.
  • A profile picture with a ring: round frames that stay visible when the app cuts the picture into a circle. See the WhatsApp DP guide.
  • A collage that tells a story: a child year by year, a couple then and now, a trip in four photos. See collage ideas.
  • A picture ready to print: both tools have a 4 × 6 inch print size, and the guides explain printing, frame sizes and how large a photo can go.

Why it is made this way

Most photo apps ask you to install something, sign in, or accept a watermark across the picture. This site does none of that. The frames and layouts are drawn by code that runs on your own phone, which is also why your photos never need to leave it. There is no account, so there is nothing to forget and nothing stored about you.

The pictures are made at 1080 pixels wide, the size phone screens and social apps show best, and at 1200 × 1800 pixels for a 4 × 6 inch print. They are saved as ordinary picture files that open in any gallery and can be sent on any app.

Good photos make good frames

The tools are quick. The part worth a minute of care is the photo itself: start from the original in your gallery, not a screenshot or a forwarded copy, and leave a little space around faces. Our photo tips and the guide to sharing without losing quality cover the rest.

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Both tools are free, with no login, no watermark and no limit on how many pictures you make.

Do you keep my photos?

No. Your photos are never sent to us. The tools work inside your browser, on your own device.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The tools are made for phones first. They also work on tablets and computers.

Can I use the pictures for my shop or page?

Yes. The frames are our own designs and you may use the pictures you make for personal or business posts.
