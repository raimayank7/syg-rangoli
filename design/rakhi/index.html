Rakhi rangoli

A rakhi drawn on the floor: a round medallion on a red thread with green beads. A sweet welcome for brothers and sisters arriving on Raksha Bandhan.

Level
Easy
Size
About 30 to 45 cm across (1 to 1.5 feet)
Time
About 15 to 20 minutes
Steps
5
Good for
Raksha Bandhan, children

Colours you need

RedYellowWhiteGreen

Tap Outline to see the design as lines, then print it to practise on paper or to colour in.

How to draw it, step by step

Sweep the floor and wipe it with a damp cloth so the powder grips. Mark the centre with chalk. Then follow the pictures. Each one adds one part.

  1. Draw the thread: one curved red line to the left of the centre and one to the right, leaving the middle empty.

  2. Make a small filled circle in yellow. This is the centre of the whole design, so place it carefully.

  3. Draw ten short pointed petals around the centre, filled in red. Mark the ten tips with dots first so the petals come out even.

  4. Place ten white dots in a ring, one in each gap. Drop a pinch of powder and press lightly for each dot.

  5. Add two green beads on the thread on each side of the medallion.

Tip: Draw the thread as one gentle curve from end to end first, then build the medallion over its middle.

Make it your own

  • Change the colours to what you have at home. Any two strong colours and white are enough.
  • Make it smaller for a puja thali or a table, or repeat it three times in a row along a wall.
  • Print the outline and let children colour it first. It is good practice before the powder comes out.
