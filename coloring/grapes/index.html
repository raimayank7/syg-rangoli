How to use this page

On paper: tap "Print the blank page". Only the picture is printed, with a line for the child's name. On screen: tap a colour, then tap a part of the picture to fill it. "Undo" takes back the last fill, and white rubs a part out. Nothing is saved; the picture is fresh each time you open it.

Colour ideas

Grapes are green, purple or black. The leaf is green and the stalk is brown. Leave a small white spot on each grape to make it shine.

Press lightly for the main colour and harder along one side to make the fruit look round. A small patch left white near the top looks like light shining on the skin. Older children can draw the bowl, basket or plate around it.

Things to know about the grapes

Read these aloud while the colouring is going on. They are short and true.

  • Grapes grow in bunches on a climbing plant called a vine.
  • Dried grapes are raisins (किशमिश).
  • Nashik in Maharashtra is famous for its grapes.
  • A bunch can have a hundred grapes.

Talk about it

  • How many grapes are in this bunch?
  • Are grapes sweet or sour?

Words to learn

In English: grapes. In Hindi: अंगूर

How hard is it?

Easy. Large, simple spaces. Suits children from about three years, and toddlers with fat crayons.
