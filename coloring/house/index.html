House colouring page

A house to print on A4 paper or to colour on the screen. Medium

How to use this page

On paper: tap "Print the blank page". Only the picture is printed, with a line for the child's name. On screen: tap a colour, then tap a part of the picture to fill it. "Undo" takes back the last fill, and white rubs a part out. Nothing is saved; the picture is fresh each time you open it.

Colour ideas

Any colours: walls, roof, door and window frames can each be different. The sun is yellow.

Straight edges are good practice for a steady hand. Colour along the inside of each line first, then fill the middle. When the picture is finished, ask who owns it or where it is going, and let the child draw that around it.

Things to know about the house

Read these aloud while the colouring is going on. They are short and true.

  • A house keeps us safe from sun, rain and cold.
  • Houses are built of brick, stone, wood, mud or concrete.
  • Sloping roofs let rain and snow slide off.
  • Around the world there are houses on stilts, on boats and made of ice.

Talk about it

  • Who lives in this house?
  • Which room do you like best at home?

Words to learn

In English: house. In Hindi: घर

How hard is it?

Medium. Some smaller parts that need a little control. Suits children from about four or five.
