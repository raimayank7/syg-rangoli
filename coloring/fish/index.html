How to use this page

On paper: tap "Print the blank page". Only the picture is printed, with a line for the child's name. On screen: tap a colour, then tap a part of the picture to fill it. "Undo" takes back the last fill, and white rubs a part out. Nothing is saved; the picture is fresh each time you open it.

Colour ideas

Any colours you like: fish are orange, gold, blue, silver, striped and spotted. Try a different colour in each stripe. The bubbles can stay white or pale blue.

Real colours are only a starting point. Ask the child what colour this animal is today and why; a blue cat with green ears has a story behind it. When the animal is done, add where it lives: grass, water, a branch, a night sky.

Things to know about the fish

Read these aloud while the colouring is going on. They are short and true.

  • Fish breathe under water with gills.
  • Fins and a tail help a fish to steer and push.
  • Most fish are covered in small, smooth scales.
  • Fish have no eyelids, so they do not blink.

Talk about it

  • Where does this fish live: a river, a pond or the sea?
  • What are the bubbles?

Words to learn

In English: fish. In Hindi: मछली

How hard is it?

Easy. Large, simple spaces. Suits children from about three years, and toddlers with fat crayons.
