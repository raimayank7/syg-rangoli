About these pages

The sun with clouds, a flower in a pot, a fruit tree, a rainbow, a mushroom, and the moon with stars. These pictures have open sky around them, which leaves room for a child to add things: birds, rain, grass, a kite.

Getting the most from them

Nature pages are good for talking about the day and the weather. Colour the sun in the morning and the moon before bed. The rainbow page is a ready-made lesson in the seven colours and their order. If the child asks why the sky is blue or where the sun goes at night, the "things to know" on each page give a short, true answer you can read out.

Which page for which age?

  • Easy: Sun and clouds, Flower in a pot, Rainbow, Mushroom
  • Medium: Fruit tree, Moon and stars

Printing

Open a picture and press "Print the blank page". The sheet fits A4 paper upright, and only the drawing and a name line are printed. If you have no printer, a child can colour the picture on the phone or tablet instead: tap a colour, then tap a space. More tips, including how to print from a phone and how to save paper, are on how to print colouring pages.
