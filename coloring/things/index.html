About these pages

A house, a car, a sailing boat, a rocket, a hot air balloon, an umbrella, balloons, a football, an ice cream cone and a teddy bear. These are the things of a child's own world and of their daydreams.

Getting the most from them

Pictures of objects have straight edges and corners, which need more control than round animals, so they are good practice for children who are getting ready to write. The house and the car have windows and wheels to colour separately. The rocket and the hot air balloon leave space above and below for drawing stars, clouds or a landscape. Ask the child who lives in the house or where the boat is going: a colouring page is the start of a story.

Which page for which age?

  • Easy: Sailing boat, Umbrella in the rain, Balloons, Ice cream cone, Teddy bear
  • Medium: House, Car, Rocket, Football, Hot air balloon

Printing

Open a picture and press "Print the blank page". The sheet fits A4 paper upright, and only the drawing and a name line are printed. If you have no printer, a child can colour the picture on the phone or tablet instead: tap a colour, then tap a space. More tips, including how to print from a phone and how to save paper, are on how to print colouring pages.
