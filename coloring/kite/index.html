Kite colouring page

How to use this page

On paper: tap "Print the blank page". Only the picture is printed, with a line for the child's name. On screen: tap a colour, then tap a part of the picture to fill it. "Undo" takes back the last fill, and white rubs a part out. Nothing is saved; the picture is fresh each time you open it.

Colour ideas

Give each of the four parts of the kite its own colour. The bows on the tail can match. Clouds stay white.

For a card or a wall decoration, bright colours with strong contrast work best: put light colours next to dark ones. A finished page can be cut out and pasted on coloured chart paper to make a greeting card.

Things to know about the kite

Read these aloud while the colouring is going on. They are short and true.

  • A kite stays up because the wind pushes against it.
  • Kites are flown at Makar Sankranti in January, at Basant, and on Independence Day in many Indian cities.
  • The city of Ahmedabad holds a big kite festival every year.
  • Fly kites in an open ground, far from roads and electric wires.

Talk about it

  • What colour is your kite?
  • When do people fly kites where you live?

Words to learn

In English: kite. In Hindi: पतंग

How hard is it?

Easy. Large, simple spaces. Suits children from about three years, and toddlers with fat crayons.
