How to use this page

On paper: tap "Print the blank page". Only the picture is printed, with a line for the child's name. On screen: tap a colour, then tap a part of the picture to fill it. "Undo" takes back the last fill, and white rubs a part out. Nothing is saved; the picture is fresh each time you open it.

Colour ideas

Colour each section of the umbrella a different colour, or use two colours in turn. Raindrops are blue. The handle is brown or black.

Straight edges are good practice for a steady hand. Colour along the inside of each line first, then fill the middle. When the picture is finished, ask who owns it or where it is going, and let the child draw that around it.

Things to know about the umbrella in the rain

Read these aloud while the colouring is going on. They are short and true.

  • An umbrella keeps off rain, and in summer it keeps off the sun too.
  • The thin rods that hold the cloth are called ribs.
  • In India the rainy season is called the monsoon.
  • Rain fills rivers and lakes and helps crops to grow.

Talk about it

  • What do you like to do in the rain?
  • What do you wear on a rainy day?

Words to learn

In English: umbrella in the rain. In Hindi: छाता

How hard is it?

Easy. Large, simple spaces. Suits children from about three years, and toddlers with fat crayons.
