Flower in a pot colouring page

A flower in a pot to print on A4 paper or to colour on the screen. Easy

How to use this page

On paper: tap "Print the blank page". Only the picture is printed, with a line for the child's name. On screen: tap a colour, then tap a part of the picture to fill it. "Undo" takes back the last fill, and white rubs a part out. Nothing is saved; the picture is fresh each time you open it.

Colour ideas

Choose any colour for the petals, a yellow or brown centre, green for the stem and leaves, and brown or red for the pot.

There is plenty of empty space around this picture. Use it: add birds as small "v" shapes, raindrops, grass along the bottom, or a border of leaves. Colouring the whole sky lightly, with the side of a crayon, makes the picture glow.

Things to know about the flower in a pot

Read these aloud while the colouring is going on. They are short and true.

  • Bees and butterflies visit flowers for a sweet drink called nectar.
  • After a flower fades, seeds grow in its place.
  • A flower needs water, soil and sunlight.
  • Some flowers open in the morning and close at night.

Talk about it

  • Which flower do you know by name?
  • What does a plant need to grow?

Words to learn

In English: flower in a pot. In Hindi: फूल

How hard is it?

Easy. Large, simple spaces. Suits children from about three years, and toddlers with fat crayons.
