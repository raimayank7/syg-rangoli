How to use this page

On paper: tap "Print the blank page". Only the picture is printed, with a line for the child's name. On screen: tap a colour, then tap a part of the picture to fill it. "Undo" takes back the last fill, and white rubs a part out. Nothing is saved; the picture is fresh each time you open it.

Colour ideas

The moon is pale yellow or white, the stars are yellow. If you colour the whole background dark blue, the picture becomes a night sky.

There is plenty of empty space around this picture. Use it: add birds as small "v" shapes, raindrops, grass along the bottom, or a border of leaves. Colouring the whole sky lightly, with the side of a crayon, makes the picture glow.

Things to know about the moon and stars

Read these aloud while the colouring is going on. They are short and true.

  • The moon has no light of its own. It shines because sunlight falls on it.
  • The moon seems to change shape through the month, from thin slice to full circle and back.
  • Stars are suns that are very, very far away.
  • India's Chandrayaan-3 landed on the moon in 2023.

Talk about it

  • What shape is the moon tonight?
  • What would you take on a trip to the moon?

Words to learn

In English: moon and stars. In Hindi: चाँद और तारे

How hard is it?

Medium. Some smaller parts that need a little control. Suits children from about four or five.
