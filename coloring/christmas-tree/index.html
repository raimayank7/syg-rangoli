Christmas tree colouring page

A Christmas tree to print on A4 paper or to colour on the screen. Medium

How to use this page

On paper: tap "Print the blank page". Only the picture is printed, with a line for the child's name. On screen: tap a colour, then tap a part of the picture to fill it. "Undo" takes back the last fill, and white rubs a part out. Nothing is saved; the picture is fresh each time you open it.

Colour ideas

The tree is green, the trunk brown, the star yellow. Colour the round ornaments and the presents in the brightest colours you have.

For a card or a wall decoration, bright colours with strong contrast work best: put light colours next to dark ones. A finished page can be cut out and pasted on coloured chart paper to make a greeting card.

Things to know about the Christmas tree

Read these aloud while the colouring is going on. They are short and true.

  • Christmas is celebrated on 25 December.
  • People decorate a tree with lights, stars and ornaments.
  • The tree is usually a fir or a pine, which stays green all winter.
  • Presents are placed under the tree.

Talk about it

  • What would you put in the two boxes?
  • Who would you give a present to?

Words to learn

In English: Christmas tree. In Hindi: क्रिसमस ट्री

How hard is it?

Medium. Some smaller parts that need a little control. Suits children from about four or five.
