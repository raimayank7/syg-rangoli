How to use this page

On paper: tap "Print the blank page". Only the picture is printed, with a line for the child's name. On screen: tap a colour, then tap a part of the picture to fill it. "Undo" takes back the last fill, and white rubs a part out. Nothing is saved; the picture is fresh each time you open it.

Colour ideas

Most owls are brown, grey or white with darker spots. The eyes are yellow or orange with a black centre, and the beak is yellow or grey.

Real colours are only a starting point. Ask the child what colour this animal is today and why; a blue cat with green ears has a story behind it. When the animal is done, add where it lives: grass, water, a branch, a night sky.

Things to know about the owl

Read these aloud while the colouring is going on. They are short and true.

  • Most owls sleep in the day and hunt at night.
  • An owl cannot move its eyes, so it turns its whole head, about three quarters of the way round.
  • Soft feathers let an owl fly almost without a sound.
  • An owl's call is a hoot.

Talk about it

  • Why does the owl have such big eyes?
  • What do you do at night?

Words to learn

In English: owl. In Hindi: उल्लू

How hard is it?

Medium. Some smaller parts that need a little control. Suits children from about four or five.
