How to use this site

  1. Pick a design that fits your space and your time. The level and the time are shown on each page.
  2. Look at the colour list and check what you have.
  3. Follow the step pictures from the centre outward.
  4. Use the outline view to practise on paper first.

Questions people ask

Are the designs free to use?

Yes. You can print them and draw them at home, at school and at events, free of charge.

Can I print a design?

Yes. Open a design, tap Outline for a line drawing, then Print. The outline is good for practice and for children to colour.

I have never made a rangoli. Where do I start?

Start with the eight petal flower on the easy page, and read the short how-to guide first. Twenty minutes is enough for a first design.

What do the levels mean?

Easy designs have four or five steps and large shapes. Medium designs have more layers. Advanced designs are large and are best made by two or three people.
